# How to edit the website

You don't need any technical knowledge, and you can't break the design.

## Log in

1. Go to **your website address, followed by `/admin`**.
2. Enter your email. You'll be sent a short code. Type it in.
3. Log in with your own editor account.

Please don't share your login with anyone.

## Change some text

1. Click the words you want to change.
2. Type in the box that opens on the left. The page updates as you type.
3. Press **Save**.
4. If a box lists possible mistakes, untick anything that's correct, such as a name. Then press **Fix it and save**, or **Save anyway**.

The change appears on the live website in about two minutes.

## Change a photo

1. Click the photo.
2. Click its small preview on the left.
3. Choose **Upload** and pick a JPG, PNG or WebP photo under 8 MB.
4. Select it, press **Insert**, then press **Save**.

The photo gets the site's colour style by itself after you save.

## Good to know

- **Italics:** put stars around the words, like `*Journal of Marketing Channels*`.
- **Facts need a source.** Timeline entries, milestones, roles and publications each need a link to the page that backs them up.
- **A red message** means something in that box needs fixing. It tells you what.
- **Check your facts before you save.** The spelling check can't tell if something is true, and nobody reviews it before the public sees it.

## If something goes wrong

Don't worry, and don't try to fix it. Every earlier version is kept, so it can be restored. Contact the person who gave you this guide.
