# How to edit the website

You don't need any technical knowledge, and you can't break the design.

## Log in

1. Go to **your website address, followed by `/admin`**.
2. Press **Log in**, then use your own editor account.

Use a strong password that you don't use anywhere else, and please don't share your login with anyone.

## Change some text

1. Click the words you want to change.
2. Type in the box that opens on the left. The page updates as you type.
3. Press **Save draft**. The spelling check runs every time.
4. If a box lists possible mistakes, untick anything that's correct, such as a name. Then press **Fix it and save**, **Edit and save** to correct it yourself, or **Save anyway**.
5. To check what you changed, press **What changed** in the bar above **Save draft**. Click any line to jump to that text in the preview.
6. When all your changes look right, press **Publish**, just above **Save draft**. They go live together in about five minutes.

Drafts stay in this browser on this computer. If the page closes or reloads, the editor offers to **Restore** them.

## Change a font

1. Click the text. Under its box on the left, choose a **Font**. The preview changes straight away, so you can flip through fonts. Nothing is public yet.
2. To keep it for only that text, press **Save draft**. Press **Undo** to go back.
3. To change every text of that kind, press the second button, such as **All section headings**. You can keep flipping through fonts, then press **Save draft for all section headings** or **Cancel**. If you saved it by mistake, press **Undo all section headings**.

The big name has its own setting and never changes with the others. Choose **Site default** to go back to the original font. If its whole kind has a font, **Same as all** follows that font and **Site default** keeps this one text original.

**To add a new font:** open **11 Site settings and sharing**, go to **Uploaded fonts**, choose a WOFF2, TTF or OTF file, press **Upload font**, **Save draft**, then **Publish**. It appears in the Font menus about five minutes later, after you refresh the page. Only upload fonts you have the right to use.

## Add something new

Every list in the editor has a **+** button next to its name: papers, books, timeline entries, roles, contact rows, sources and gallery photos.

1. Open the section, for example **05 Research and teaching**, and find the list, such as **Selected papers**.
2. Press **+**. A new item appears at the end of the list. Click it and fill in its boxes.
3. Drag the dots on its left to move it higher or lower.
4. Press **Save draft**, then **Publish**.

Papers, books, roles and table rows need a source link starting with https://. The editor won't save one without it.

**Gallery photos:** press **+** next to **Photos**, upload the photo, then add a description, caption and credit. The gallery holds up to 21 photos, and its layout repeats every seven. **Introduction** adds an optional short paragraph under the gallery heading.

## Change a photo

1. Click the photo.
2. Click its small preview on the left.
3. Choose **Upload** and pick a JPG, PNG or WebP photo under 8 MB.
4. Select it, press **Insert**, then press **Save draft** and **Publish**.

The photo gets the site's colour style by itself after you save.

## Delete something

Click the text and press **Delete** under its box. It leaves the page and the spacing closes up. **Restore** brings it back. Press **Save draft**, then **Publish** when you are happy.

To delete a whole item, such as a timeline entry, paper or photo, open its list and press the bin icon beside it. A section disappears once everything in it is deleted.

## Hide the small source lines

Open **11 Site settings and sharing** and turn on **Hide sources and photo credits**. Press **Save draft**, then **Publish**. The lines stay saved, so turning the switch off brings them back.

To keep a few while the rest are hidden, click that source or credit and turn on **Show even when sources are hidden**.

To change the statement in the dark footer, open **10 Footer** and edit **Footer notice**.

## Good to know

- **Italics:** put stars around the words, like `*Journal of Marketing Channels*`.
- **Facts need a source.** Timeline entries, milestones, roles and publications each need a link to the page that backs them up.
- **A red message** means something in that box needs fixing. It tells you what.
- **Check your facts before you save.** The spelling check can't tell if something is true, and nobody reviews it before the public sees it.

## If something goes wrong

Don't worry, and don't try to fix it. Every earlier version is kept, so it can be restored. Contact the person who gave you this guide.
